Why dancers use it

Fuel like it's part of training, not an afterthought.

Long rehearsal days, back-to-back shows and early calls ask a lot of your body. Encore helps you match fuelling and recovery to what the week actually demands, so class, rehearsal and performance all feel steadier, across the whole season.

Energy for the whole day

Two shows back to back, a late rehearsal, an early call time. Encore helps you plan around your actual schedule, not a generic meal plan.

Fewer repeat niggles

Under-fuelling shows up as bodies that tire, ache or get injured more easily. Encore helps you catch the pattern before it costs you a role.

A cycle that works with your training

Your cycle affects energy, strength and recovery. Encore tracks it alongside your schedule, built for dance, not generic fitness.

How it works

From a weekly check-in, to a plan you can trust.

Two minutes a week, read by your copilot, with a real person deciding anything that matters.

01

Weekly check-in

Quick questions on fuelling, energy, cycle and any niggles, plus whatever your wearable already knows. No food diaries, no numbers.

↓
02

Encore reads the pattern

Load, cycle, sleep and fuelling, read together over time, not any one answer on its own.

↓
03

You keep going

Fuelling and recovery guidance for the week ahead, and your next check-in picks up where this one left off. Most weeks end here.

↺ Back to your weekly check-in

03

A practitioner takes a look

Reviewed by a person, not an algorithm

A registered dietitian or your care team sees a clear, structured summary, never raw numbers or food logs, and decides what happens next.

↓
04

Recovery, if it's needed

If something's confirmed, you move into recovery mode. Encore tracks what matters: energy, sleep, mood, training tolerance and whether your cycle returns to normal, until you're back to full training.

Designed for dancers, with care.

No calorie counting

We talk about fuelling and timing, never numbers on a plate.

No weigh ins

Encore never asks for or shows your weight.

Evidence first

Guidance is grounded in the IOC consensus on REDs and the validated Dance Energy Availability Questionnaire (DEAQ), reviewed by registered dietitians.

Your data is yours

Nothing is shared with your school or company unless you say so.

For clinicians and schools

See the whole week, not just the appointment.

Roll Encore out as part of your welfare or dance health programme, and dancers use it the way they'd turn up for a physio screening, not as a personal admission that something's wrong. Dietitians and physios get consented, structured summaries between sessions.
